Good to know.

Does Sketch behave exactly like real paper?

It models fixed and loose pigment, tilt contact, calibrated pressure response, paper tooth and burnishing, virtual tip wear, finger pickup/deposit and two eraser types. It remains a digital approximation: e-ink latency and the stylus surface affect the feel. Calibration adjusts sensor response; it is not a physical-pencil comparison or an optical latency measurement. The screen uses fine black-and-white halftoning for responsive drawing; saved artwork and PNG/PDF exports retain grayscale. Save manually before closing or updating.

Is Web a full Chrome replacement?

No. It simplifies static HTML for reading and does not run page JavaScript. Login-heavy sites, video and web apps are not supported. Public HTTPS PDF/EPUB downloads are supported up to 64 MB, with at least 576 MB free before starting. Other file formats are not saved. Images are optional; public image URLs are processed by images.weserv.nl without forwarding cookies or login details.

How are SSH and RDP passwords stored?

Paper Terminal can remember connection details and, optionally, passwords. Saved SSH passwords use private local files without encryption. Its terminal engine needs no LiTerm runtime.

For RDP, you can disable Remember password for each profile. Saved passwords are locally obfuscated, not encrypted; someone with root access to the tablet can recover them. Use only computers and accounts you trust.

Will Books find every book or paper?

No. Choose from six sources in Settings. Catalogue coverage, language and full-text access vary. Open Library only offers unrestricted editions; arXiv entries are preprints, not necessarily peer reviewed. Books does not bypass paywalls, borrowing restrictions or DRM. PDF/EPUB editions appear in My files; Gutenberg and Europe PMC text stays in Books.

Do you collect app telemetry?

PaperDesk has no app telemetry or analytics SDK. Apps contact the services you choose to use. This website records aggregate download totals by file, day and estimated country, not IP addresses in the statistics database. Server access logs are separate.
