Already installed? Updates & missing apps

Use Care for signed updates. PaperDesk does not enable boot-time XOVI injection. After a restart, start XOVI deliberately; after a firmware update, you may need to reinstall the apps.

Recovery instructions ↗ · Care & updates ↗
What changed in 4.2.8?

Paper Sketch has a faster leading trace for rapid strokes and tiny marks on the tested OS 3.29.0.153 build. Finished artwork keeps material detail, pressure, tilt and full-resolution exports.

Privacy, limitations & licences

No app analytics SDK. Apps contact services you choose. This website records aggregate downloads by file, day and estimated country; the statistics database does not store IP addresses. Downloads are not unique users.

Web is a simplified reader, not a full browser. SSH passwords may be stored in private local files without encryption; remembered RDP passwords are obfuscated, not encrypted. Sketch is a digital approximation with e-ink latency. Save your artwork manually.

Component licences and source are included in the package and repository. This is independent software, not affiliated with or endorsed by reMarkable or OpenAI.
