1 November 2026 · Version 4.1.3

Materials beside
your drawing tools.

Paper Sketch 4.1.3 puts Materials settings first in the middle toolbar, before the 2H pencil. Open it for the six controls for exploring dry media. Each is a digital simulation; the tablet surface and e-ink panel still shape the drawing experience.

Strokes start at initial pen contact. Manual saves and grayscale PNG exports stay familiar. Read the Sketch guide ↗

  • Tilt and contact shape.Tilted graphite and charcoal form directional marks when sensor tilt is available. Missing tilt uses an upright tip.
  • Pressure calibration.Record light, normal and comfortably firm lines on a separate practice pad. The response shapes width and pigment deposition without claiming a match to a measured physical pencil.
  • Paper and burnishing.Plain, Smooth and Rough change future contact. Firm marks gradually smooth simulated tooth; existing pigment stays in place.
  • Wear and sharpening.Drawing gradually broadens and flattens the virtual tip. Sharpen restores the selected tool's point.
  • Finger contact and cleaning.Blend with a variable contact shape, carry pigment between marks, and use Clean finger to clear that carried pigment.
  • Hard and kneaded erasers.Rub along a path with the hard eraser. The kneaded eraser lifts at the first touch: lift and dab again to repeat.

Good to know.

Does Sketch behave exactly like real paper?

It models fixed and loose pigment, tilt contact, calibrated pressure response, paper tooth and burnishing, virtual tip wear, finger pickup/deposit and two eraser types. It remains a digital approximation: e-ink latency and the stylus surface affect the feel. Calibration adjusts sensor response; it is not a physical-pencil comparison or an optical latency measurement. The screen uses fine black-and-white halftoning for responsive drawing; saved artwork and PNG exports retain grayscale. Save manually before closing or updating.

Is Web a full Chrome replacement?

No. It simplifies static HTML for reading and does not run page JavaScript. Login-heavy sites, video and web apps are not supported. Public HTTPS PDF/EPUB downloads are supported up to 64 MB, with at least 576 MB free before starting. Other file formats are not saved. Images are optional; public image URLs are processed by images.weserv.nl without forwarding cookies or login details.

Does RDP save my password securely?

You can disable Remember password for each profile. Saved passwords are locally obfuscated, not encrypted; someone with root access to the tablet can recover them. Use only computers and accounts you trust.

Will Books find every book or paper?

No. Choose from six sources in Settings. Catalogue coverage, language and full-text access vary. Open Library only offers unrestricted editions; arXiv entries are preprints, not necessarily peer reviewed. Books does not bypass paywalls, borrowing restrictions or DRM. PDF/EPUB editions appear in My files; Gutenberg and Europe PMC text stays in Books.

Do you collect app telemetry?

PaperDesk has no app telemetry or analytics SDK. Apps contact the services you choose to use. This website records aggregate download totals by file, day and estimated country, not IP addresses in the statistics database. Server access logs are separate.
