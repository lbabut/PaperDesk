On the actual tablet

Every control earns its place.

These are real reMarkable 2 captures from physical touch tests—not desktop mock-ups. Controls stay visible when they prevent friction; secondary actions stay grouped and out of the reading area.

What we deliberately did not add

No permanent browser keyboard, decorative toolbars, animated feedback, hidden edge gestures, or duplicate Home buttons. If an action does not help connection, navigation, reading, or recovery, it does not get screen space.

Why PaperDesk

Made for the device you own.

PaperDesk does not pretend an e-ink tablet is a fast LCD. It turns those limits into a calmer way to check a server, read a page, or reach a remote computer.

Deliberate refresh

Updates are batched and triggered when they matter, reducing needless flashing and ghosting.

Touch you can trust

Stable targets and physical-tablet journeys exercise taps, swipes, pinches, keyboard input, and close flows.

Local by default

No PaperDesk account and no telemetry. Connection profiles and reading data stay on the tablet.

Open source, for real

Useful first. Sustainable second.

PaperDesk stays free to use, inspect, modify, and share under its component licences. There are no paid features, accounts, telemetry, ads, or artificial limits.
