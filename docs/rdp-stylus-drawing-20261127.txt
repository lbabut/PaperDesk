Defect: pollInput normalized pen coordinates but ignored PEN_PRESS/UPDATE,
then treated PEN_RELEASE as a click. AppLoad's actual FBController source
maps mouse/stylus press/move/release to these three PEN packet types.
This is a functional input-path defect, not proof of a slow framebuffer.

Change: dedicated contact-only stylus path sends immediate left-down, every
move, and final move/up. Hover/unmatched releases do not click. Palm touch
packets cannot steal an active pen stroke. Keyboard/viewport/toolbar boundary
and disconnect resets release the held button. Finger gestures, local zoom,
legacy toolbar and certificate/profile handling retain their existing paths.
No frame cadence, quality, remote resolution or pressure protocol changed:
RDP receives a mouse stroke, not pressure-sensitive Windows Ink.
