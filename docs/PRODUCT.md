# Product brief

## One-line promise

PaperDesk turns reMarkable 2 into a quiet network workspace: SSH, reader-first
web, RDP, and RSS without animations, noisy refresh loops, or desktop clutter.

## Audience

The first customer is a technically confident reMarkable 2 owner who uses the
tablet every day and wants focused access to a server, workstation, or reading
queue. Early niches: developers, sysadmins, researchers, writers, homelab
owners, and accessibility-minded e-ink enthusiasts.

## Why it wins

PaperDesk is not another launcher or collection of unrelated ports. It is one
tested workflow with a consistent touch UI, a shared e-ink refresh philosophy,
an installer with backups, and real-device regression tests.

## Sustainability

PaperDesk remains free and open source. Voluntary support funds compatibility
work, real-device testing, documentation, and maintenance. Optional guided
setup can be offered separately without locking features or updates behind a
payment.

## Current boundary

Release 1.2.1 is validated only on reMarkable 2 with reMarkable OS 3.29.0.149,
XOVI 0.3.3, AppLoad 0.6.0, rm-xovi-extensions v19, and LiTerm 0.1.7. Public
copy must say this clearly and must not imply official support from reMarkable.
