PaperDesk RDP now keeps a touch-friendly list of up to eight recent connection profiles.
Each profile remembers Scale (50%, 75%, or 100%) and Colors (256, 65K, or 16M).
Lower settings negotiate fewer remote pixels and less colour data for a faster e-ink session; 75% and 256 colours are recommended.
Scaled sessions fill the screen and keep clicks, double-clicks, right-clicks, pan, and pinch aligned.
