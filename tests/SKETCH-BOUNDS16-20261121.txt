EXPERIMENT: ELLIPSE BOUNDS + 16-INPUT GUI TURN

Based on 0b1d759, not main/release. Same conservative charcoal bounds and
same material/pressure/spacing/publication, with takePenInput capped at16
instead of24. No sample dropping or thinner/fainter rendering.
The exact600-point input-fidelity test now explicitly requires <=16 events
and38 batches for601 events, rather than26 batches at24. Its original
old-contract assertion failed as expected, then the full suite was rerun.
32 C++ plus2 C regression executables passed. Five report-validation and
six installer tests passed. Actual finite tilt is checked in the report,
not just the requested mode. Product checksum verification passed.
Material state was previously exact against frozen input24 after8100 dabs
and traces on host+ARM; material header is unchanged in this16-input variant.

All device cohorts used1260 injected pen samples, real production handlers,
isolated no-save namespaces, existing saved-setting/cache preservation
checks, and automatic config/UI restoration. Not a physical panel/stylus
latency measurement, not a full library audit. No production publication.
CPU governor/load were not controlled; small sequential cohorts, no claim
of precise universal speed ratio. Before-run temperature25C; CPU snapshots
mostly1.2GHz, one16-input repeat996MHz. Raw environments retained.

DECISION: not accepted as an all-tool responsiveness fix and not retained
on the tablet. The heavy-case improvement does not pass strict budgets;
standard graphite has a147ms outlier versus48ms in the first new input24
control. Three runs do not establish the cause of that outlier, but do not
hide it or call the change regression-free. Restore input24 be89afb1.
Further work should isolate expensive GUI-turn work rather than simply
shrinking batches and moving latency into the queue. No new owner action
is needed for the completed test; no claim of continuous background work.
