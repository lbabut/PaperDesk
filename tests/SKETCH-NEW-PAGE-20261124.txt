New page capacity UX regression, 2026-11-24

Baseline: 802925a227811c4046e54d7f14fe9813e4f96270, installed rowspan source.
Read-only device diagnosis: active notebook has 12 pages, index 11.
The pre-existing capacity of 12 is enforced in QML, the JavaScript decoder
and native persistence. No capacity or saved-format change in this patch.

Previously New page on a full dirty book requested Save/Discard before
failing with a small footer notice. It could therefore save or discard
unnecessarily even though the requested page could not be added.

Now capacity is checked before that prompt, with a non-destructive modal
and a single Back to drawing action. Defensive execution also checks the
limit. Unknown confirmation kinds cannot fall through to redraw/dirty state.
The message distinguishes Delete page from Clear page; no deletion is
performed automatically. PDF export is a rendered copy, not an editable
material/sketchbook backup.

Host QML tests cover full saved and unsaved notebooks through the real
button, unchanged notebook/index/dirty/saved document, absence of save,
discard and destructive actions, defensive dispatch, and creating page 12.
Complete Sketch suite: 71 passed, 0 failed, 2 skipped (tablet native runtime
required). Offscreen screenshot full-sketchbook.png visually checked.
