METHOD
Read /proc/PID/task/*/stat and schedstat before and after synthetic injection.
No affinity, scheduling priority, governor or framebuffer policy changes.
Task birth time protects against TID reuse. A new task contributes cumulative
CPU only if its birth is provably after the complete first snapshot; otherwise
it remains explicitly unavailable, never zero. Vanished tasks are recorded.
Scheduling wait is runnable time, not mutex sleep; different tasks' waits
overlap and must not be added as elapsed duration. Names alone do not establish
worker roles. The main PID is explicitly recorded in later reports.

Initial shell sampler A1 cost300..760ms per scan and is EXCLUDED from useful
low-overhead evidence. Its raw report is retained. Replacement single-process
C sampler took0..20ms per scan at10ms uptime resolution, not literal zero cost.
Windows include SSH/driver startup/reporting and some release tail, not an
